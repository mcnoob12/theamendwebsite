Who we are

About

An idealistic and dreamy Future Technology Corporation.

Amend. is a small, independent team building technology around one question: what would this look like if it were made for the person using it, rather than for whoever profits from them?

Right now that means a handful of projects. One helps fans of the same niche find each other nearby. One lets websites use the AI running on your own computer. One looks for connections between ideas from fields that never talk to each other. Some are live, some are experiments, and we keep them free wherever we can.

The way we build matters as much as what we build: private by default, clear about how things work, and honest about the people whose work and data make it possible.

We're early, and we're trying to make a real change. Maybe we'll succeed, maybe we won't.

Time will tell.

Ideas

Now

Today, the most important thing is an idea.

Making things has never been cheaper. Code gets written in an afternoon, a prototype ships in a weekend, and a machine will draft almost anything you ask it for.

What hasn't gotten cheaper is knowing what's worth making. Noticing a real need, or seeing that two things nobody put together belong together: that's the scarce part now. So that's where we put our time.

What we believe

Belief

Powerful technology should be available to everyone.

Not only to whoever can pay the most, and never at the expense of the people inside it. Every system has humans behind it: the people who use it, the people whose work and data it runs on, and the people it leaves out.

We try to build for all of them. For example:

  • NicheNetFree and non-commercial. Your exact location never leaves your phone.
  • LocalLinkIf your computer can run an AI model, the choice of model stays yours.
  • DerivativeEvery new idea keeps a record of the people and ideas it came from.

Mission

Technology, with people kept at the center.

Our mission is simple: to amend how technology is imagined, designed and experienced, so that it serves people with more care.

Too much of big tech treats people as the product. We want to be the alternative: driven by values instead of greed, private by design, and built for the people who actually use it.

N

NicheNet

nn.amend.co.in
AlphaAI-assisted

Find people near you who love the same niche things you do, like a small artist such as Jamie Paige. Pick your niches or import them from Last.fm, see how many fans live in your neighbourhood, city, state or country, and join the group chat for that niche and area.

You choose how precisely your area is shared. It's free, and built only to help fans connect.

Open NicheNet
L

LocalLink

kalaris-locallink
v0.1AI-assisted

A protocol that lets websites use AI models running on your own computer. Choose "Local model" on a site, approve it once, and it can talk to your models in Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, Jan or any OpenAI-compatible server.

If you have the hardware, you get the choice, and your conversations stay on your machine.

Open protocol, MIT licensed
D

Derivative

Discovery engine
Test buildAI-assisted

Finds structural connections between ideas developed independently in different fields, like counterpoint in music and concurrent processes in computing, and shows where each one came from.

It's built as an honest test: if connections from a shared pool of ideas don't beat brainstorming alone with an AI, the thesis fails, and that's worth knowing too.
